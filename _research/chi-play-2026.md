My work at [CHI PLAY 2026](https://chiplay.acm.org/2026/) in York included a Perspectives on Play paper, a collaborative 
Work in Progress paper, and a workshop on inclusive games and controllers. Each contribution explored how gaming hardware,
platform rules, and design constraints can shape access to play.

## The Accessibility Paradox

[_The Accessibility Paradox: Platform Standardisation and the Legitimacy of Play_](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3800965.3834658)
is a sole-authored Perspectives on Play paper that examines how platform standards can make accessible play possible while
also limiting players' ability to adapt hardware or develop their own solutions; this was based on common participant experiences
of other works in progress. I argue that improving accessibility requires us to focus more on who sets the rules for which 
adaptations are accepted, rather than just the technical work of making games and controllers usable.

## Designing with Impaired Technologies

I assisted on [_Designing with Impaired Technologies: A Game Jam of Constraints and Affordance Shrinkage_](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3800965.3834287),
which was presented as a Work in Progress paper. The research used damaged gaming equipment as a design focus point for a two-day
public game jam, where participants played existing games before designing their own games in response to hardware constraints. 
The study explored how players adapt when their available interactions are restricted, and how these adaptations can
inspire new game designs. Themes included turning faults into features and building constraints into gameplay.

## Playing with Constraints

I co-organised [_Playing with Constraints: Designing Inclusive Games and Controllers_](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3800965.3834573).
Building on the impaired technologies research, the workshop explored how constraints could inspire games and
controllers for players with different abilities. The activities centred on playing with constrained hardware, to provoke 
development of multiplayer game concepts or physical prototypes that involve constraints. The workshop connected questions 
about impaired technology with the design of inclusive play experiences.
