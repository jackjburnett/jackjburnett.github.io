Notable outputs from widening participation, inclusive education, and school governance

Talks and Workshops

I’m open to invitations to give talks, run workshops, or take part in Q&A sessions. If you’re organising something for a school, community group, or another audience, feel free to get in touch.

The Vegan Society 2026 Education Resources

As an Education Network volunteer, I contributed to Including vegan learners during the festive season and We do not need to use non-human animals to learn; both of these resources are aimed at promoting teachers to provide vegan-inclusive learning environments.

FutureQuest Session

As part of my role as a widening participation tutor, I was invited to deliver a 75-minute introductory session on artificial intelligence to year 10 students through the FutureQuest programme. The session introduced students to AI. Through interactive activities, students explored how machines learn via supervised and unsupervised learning, attempting to cluster and analyse data themselves before comparing their results with AI systems. The lesson also examined real-world applications, the benefits of AI, issues such as bias, and what responsible AI use looks like in practice.

Access to Bristol: Cryptography in Python 2025 & 2026

As part of my role as a widening participation tutor, I helped run a Cryptography in Python session for the Access to Bristol programme, led by Yolan Ankaine. I continued this role in 2026.

Maple Hub Opening

I attended the formal opening of the Maple Hub at Hans Price Academy, where I serve as SEND Link Governor. The hub provides specialist support for students with EHCPs whose primary needs relate to speech, language, and communication or autism.

Cats Protection Talks

I deliver Cats Protection talks in Bristol and North Somerset. The talks are free for schools and community groups of all ages; they cover topics such as responsible pet ownership and careers working with cats. The sessions can be tailored to your group or fit them into your curriculum.

I’m a Computer Scientist

I take part in I’m a Computer Scientist, answering students’ questions about computing and helping them explore STEM careers and research.

The Controller Project

I previously volunteered with The Controller Project, printing and assembling controller mods for disabled gamers in the UK. If you need a controller mod to make gaming more accessible, the Caleb is really helpful and can talk through what might work for you.
